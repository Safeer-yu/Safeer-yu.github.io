Book Summarizer: multi-agent LLM pipeline In progress

Takes a full book plus inputs (purpose, audience, length) and produces a summary tailored to them. Currently built and tested on Arabic books.

Repo

Large-scale word embeddings and semantic analysis

Research · UAE University

Trained Word2Vec models on a ~1.9-billion-word Arabic corpus on the university HPC cluster, tuning parameters across multiple runs.

Used them to study the semantic coherence of Arabic root families: a root-extraction pipeline combining the Farasa stemmer and the Aralex lexical resource, then unweighted and frequency-weighted coherence scores from pairwise cosine similarity.

  • HPC
  • Farasa
  • Aralex

Neural-network modelling of transposed-letter priming in Arabic

Research

Neural-network simulations of the transposed-letter (TL) priming effect in Arabic, comparing transposed vs. substituted letter conditions and building on Lerner et al. (2014).

Models map letter identity, letter form (allography) and position inputs to orthographic or semantic (fastText) representations.
