Repo

Semantic coherence of Arabic root families

Research · UAE University

How closely related in meaning are Arabic words that share a root? Built a ~1.9-billion-word Arabic corpus, cleaned it, and extracted roots by combining Farasa stems with the Aralex lexicon. Words sharing a root were grouped into families.

Trained Word2Vec models on the university HPC cluster and scored each family’s semantic coherence from pairwise cosine similarity (unweighted and frequency-weighted).

Next In progress: a lexical decision megastudy with human participants, testing whether root-family coherence affects how quickly Arabic words are recognized.

Repo

Transposed-letter priming and allography in Arabic: neural-network simulations In progress

Research · UAE University

Investigates whether the transposed-letter (TL) priming effect in Arabic is modulated by allography, since Arabic letters change shape depending on their position in the word.

Built neural-network simulations that compare transposed vs. substituted letter primes, with inputs encoding letter identity, letter form and position, mapped to orthographic or semantic representations. Results are still being analyzed.
